VenueTuner — installation
=========================

1. Drag VenueTuner into the Applications folder.

2. First launch (only once per version):
   VenueTuner is free and not sold through the App Store, so macOS can't
   verify the developer with Apple. Open it once like this:

   • macOS 15 Sequoia and later:
     Double-click VenueTuner → click "Done" on the warning →
     System Settings › Privacy & Security → scroll down →
     "VenueTuner was blocked…" → Open Anyway → confirm.

   • macOS 13–14:
     Right-click (or Ctrl-click) VenueTuner in Applications → Open → Open.

   Alternative (Terminal, one line):
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VenueTuner.app

3. When asked, allow microphone access: VenueTuner measures your system
   through the audio interface and the measurement microphone.

Requirements: macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
Settings, snapshots, presets: ~/Library/Application Support/VenueTuner
