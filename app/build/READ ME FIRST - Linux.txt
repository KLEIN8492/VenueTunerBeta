VenueTuner for Linux - installation
===================================

AppImage (any distribution):
  chmod +x VenueTuner-*.AppImage
  ./VenueTuner-*.AppImage
  (Ubuntu 22.04+ may need: sudo apt install libfuse2)

Without AppImage (tar.gz):
  tar xzf VenueTuner-*.tar.gz && ./VenueTuner-*/venuetuner


Audio:
  - ALSA works out of the box: choose your interface in Settings > Audio.
    Close other apps using it, or use JACK / PipeWire-JACK.
  - JACK (or PipeWire's JACK layer) is used when it is running.
  - For low latency without dropouts, add yourself to the audio group and
    allow real-time priority:
      sudo usermod -aG audio $USER     (log out and in again)

Requirements: 64-bit x86 Linux, glibc 2.34+ (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 35 or newer).
Settings, snapshots, presets: ~/.config/VenueTuner
